# Welcome to Tractus-X Accelerator

Tractus-X Accelerator brings people and projects together to explore, build, and scale solutions with the Tractus-X open-source ecosystem.

## Project Partners

- Arena2036 e.V.
- Catena-X e.V.
- Fraunhofer ISST
- International Data Spaces Association e.V.

This project is funded by the German Federal Ministry of Economics and Energy (BMWE) with a funding base of 7,5 Million Euros for 3 years (Sep 2026 - Sep 2029).

## Explore Tractus-X

Visit the [Eclipse Tractus-X organization on GitHub](https://github.com/eclipse-tractusx) to discover its projects, code, and documentation.

## Get involved

We welcome organizations, developers, and community members interested in contributing. Explore the repositories in this organization to find project resources, contribution guidance, and ways to connect with the teams behind the work.
